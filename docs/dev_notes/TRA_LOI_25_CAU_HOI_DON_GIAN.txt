CAU 6: THUAT TOAN YOLO v11 LA GI? CHUC NANG?

Dinh nghia:
YOLO (You Only Look Once) la thuat toan phat hien doi tuong (object detection) su dung Deep Learning. Phien ban 11 phat trien boi Ultralytics (10/2024).

Kien truc:
- Input Image (640x640)
- BACKBONE (CSPDarknet): Feature Extraction
- NECK (PANet): Multi-scale Features
- HEAD (Detect): Predictions
- Output: [x, y, w, h, confidence, class]

Chuc nang trong de tai:
- Phat hien phuong tien (car, motorcycle, bus, truck)
- Toc do: khoang 200 FPS (YOLO v11n tren GPU)
- Do chinh xac: tren 90% mAP

Tai sao chon YOLO:
- One-stage detector nen nhanh
- End-to-end training nen don gian
- Open source va Active community

CAU 7: THUAT TOAN BYTETRACK LA GI? CHUC NANG?

Dinh nghia:
ByteTrack la thuat toan Multi-Object Tracking (MOT) - theo doi nhieu doi tuong qua video, phat trien nam 2021 boi ByteDance.

Muc dich: Gan ID duy nhat cho moi xe, theo doi xe qua nhieu frames

Van de giai quyet:
- Khong tracking: Frame 1: 3 xe, Frame 2: 3 xe, Frame 3: 4 xe. Tong = 10 (SAI)
- Co tracking: Frame 1: Xe A, B, C. Frame 2: Xe A, B, C (same IDs). Frame 3: Xe A, B, C, D. Tong = 4 xe (DUNG)

Thuat toan:
1. Kalman Filter: Du doan vi tri xe o frame tiep theo
2. IoU Matching: Tinh do overlap giua predicted va detected boxes
3. Hungarian Algorithm: Optimal assignment

Chuc nang trong de tai:
- Dem xe chinh xac (khong dem trung)
- Tinh toc do dua tren trajectory
- Phat hien vi pham

CAU 18: DANG STREAMING (KAFKA, MQTT) LA GI?

Dinh nghia:
Streaming la phuong thuc truyen du lieu lien tuc, real-time giua cac thanh phan he thong.

Cac cong nghe streaming:
- Kafka: Message broker phan tan, dung cho Big data, log processing
- MQTT: Protocol nhe cho IoT, dung cho Sensor, thiet bi nhung
- WebSocket: Full-duplex qua HTTP, dung cho Web real-time
- RabbitMQ: Message queue don gian, dung cho Task queue

Trong de tai nay:
- Su dung WebSocket cho video streaming
- Co the upgrade len Kafka khi scale lon (100+ cameras)
